# Copy

## Hero statement — not yet chosen

Formula the idiom uses: *[Name] is a [kind of body] that [does a specific thing].*
Noun first, no benefit claim.

1. **In the prototype now.**
   Andrea Fanelli is an independent designer who works with founders on the
   objects they haven't made yet.

2. Andrea Fanelli is a designer working at the stage where a product is still
   a feeling.

3. Independent designer. Objects, and the arguments behind them.

Rejected: anything opening "I help…", "I partner with…", "Crafting…".
Note that ÆHD Lab already owns "design for things that don't exist yet" — this
site cannot reuse it.

## Sub-line (under the hero)

> Product, brand and creative direction for early-stage hardware and consumer
> technology. Everything below is in one list: the objects and the thinking that
> produced them.

The second sentence is doing real work — it explains the register before the
visitor hits it. Keep it or replace it with something that does the same job.

## Status line

> Rome, Italy — Available from Jan 2027

Placeholder, and a decision. Options: name a date, say "Taking projects for
[quarter]", or say nothing at all. Saying nothing is safest while still employed.

## Information / practice

> I design products before they are products — the stage where a thing is still
> a feeling, a deck and a rough foam model. Most of the work is hardware and the
> world it needs around it: the object, the name, the story, the film that
> announces it.
>
> I work with founders who have just raised and are building something tangible.
> Small teams, long conversations, few meetings. Projects are priced whole, not
> by the day.

## Capabilities — plain list, never icons in a grid

Product & industrial design · Brand & identity · Creative direction ·
Launch films & pitch material · Research, trends & signals

## Colophon

> Designing from Rome

Follows Apple's "Designed by Apple in California" / Ellis Hamburger's
"WRITTEN IN LITTLE TOKYO · LOS ANGELES". Same line as ÆHD's "DESIGNING FROM
ITALY", so consider differentiating if both sites ship.

## Retired from the old site

> A curious mind exploring the edges of all that's possible with design and beyond.

Too soft and says nothing a founder can act on. Kept here in case a fragment of
it is worth salvaging.

> I believe great design is about creating a feeling. A powerful tool for
> communicating and shaping ideas. Not just a way to create objects but
> relationships that connect things, environments and people.

Opens with "I believe", which the idiom bans. The idea underneath — design as
feeling, not object — is good and could be rewritten declaratively.
